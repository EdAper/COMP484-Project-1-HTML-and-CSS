Howlin' Ray's

Howlin' Ray's is a Nashville hot chicken restaurant located inside Via Via Food Hall at The Venetian. It is a great place to visit if you enjoy spicy chicken sandwiches, crispy chicken, and flavorful sauces.

My recommendation would be to try one of their hot chicken sandwiches and choose a spice level that matches your taste.

Visit Howlin' Ray's

Sadelle's Cafe

Sadelle's Cafe is located inside the Bellagio and is known for breakfast, brunch, and lunch. The restaurant has an elegant atmosphere and views of the Bellagio Conservatory.

Sadelle's is especially known for dishes such as bagels, French toast, salads, and other classic brunch foods.

Visit Sadelle's Cafe

El Dorado Cantina

El Dorado Cantina is a Mexican restaurant located on the Las Vegas Strip. It offers a lively atmosphere and a variety of Mexican dishes made with fresh ingredients.

It is a good choice when you want flavorful dinner, handmade tortillas, tacos, enchiladas, or other Mexican food.

Visit El Dorado Cantina

Other Food Experiences

Las Vegas has many other food experiences besides individual restaurants. Visitors can enjoy hotel buffets, food halls, dessert shops, celebrity chef restaurants, and late-night food throughout the city.

  • Hotel buffets with many different food choices
  • Food halls with several restaurants in one location
  • Restaurants with views of the Las Vegas Strip
  • Late-night restaurants and snack spots
  • Desserts, bakeries, and specialty coffee shops
