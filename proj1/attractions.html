The Las Vegas Strip

The Las Vegas Strip is one of the most famous areas in the city. It is filled with hotels, casinos, restaurants, shops, lights, and entertainment.

Fremont Street Experience

Fremont Street is located in downtown Las Vegas. It is known for its bright LED canopy, live entertainment, zip lines, and classic casino atmosphere.

Bellagio Fountains

The Bellagio Fountains are a popular free attraction. Water, music, and lights are combined into a large performance in front of the Bellagio hotel.
