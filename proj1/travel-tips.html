Las Vegas Travel Tips

Helpful advice for planning a Las Vegas trip

Plan Your Budget

Las Vegas offers many different types of experiences, so it is important to plan a budget before traveling. Hotels, food, entertainment, transportation, and activities can become expensive quickly.

Wear Comfortable Shoes

The Las Vegas Strip is much larger than it may appear. Walking between hotels, restaurants, shops, and attractions can take a long time, so comfortable shoes are very important.

Stay Hydrated

Las Vegas can be extremely hot, especially during the summer. Visitors should carry water, use sunscreen, and take breaks while exploring the city.

Plan Entertainment Ahead of Time

Popular shows, concerts, restaurants, and attractions can sell out. Making reservations or purchasing tickets in advance can help prevent problems during your trip.

Respect Casino Rules

Casinos have rules about age, photography, gambling, and behavior. Visitors should follow the rules and remember that gambling should always be done responsibly.
